Credit for traders, with the risk left visible.

Margin Line screens trading history before offering credit. Its contracts keep borrowed funds inside the trading account and distribute losses through a reserve, junior lenders, then senior lenders.

Recorded evidence explorer. This page makes no wallet, exchange or blockchain connection. It reads saved backtests and a local-fork stress replay.

What if the keeper arrives late?

A keeper freezes and settles an unhealthy credit line. This saved local-fork experiment replays the selected historical day under four credit and keeper conditions.

Replay conditions include a controlled order book and worst allowed unwind prices. This is a recorded fork result, not a new market simulation or a guarantee for deposited funds.
